# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Malaysian car shoppers browsing on phones, job applicants, and staff maintaining dealership content.

## Product Purpose
Help visitors discover BYD vehicles, inspect real colour photography, compare two catalogue entries, and contact their preferred branch. Let applicants submit a résumé with a preferred branch.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Express, Node 24+ and SQLite. Preserve published catalogue facts, legacy specifications, authentication, CSRF, and private résumés. Comparisons operate on catalogue entries, without nested trims. Event orientation warnings are advisory. English UI. Redeploy to the existing server on port 8082 with a copied-data preflight, backup and rollback.

## Brand Commitments
BYD Millennium Motors identity and authentic WordPress imagery. Cinematic vehicle-led public showroom with light editorial surfaces, Sora typography, the official BYD mark, restrained BYD red accents and large authentic photography. The manager retains its dark interface. Forms live on focused pages. No fabricated specs or digitally recoloured cars.

## Evidence on Hand
Repository content and public https://millenniumautobyd.com.my/home/ media. Initial branch source: public contact page, excluding the coming-soon location. Existing managed records take precedence.

## Product Principles
- Make phone browsing and side-by-side decisions comfortable.
- Keep all customer-facing facts editable by staff.
- Keep publication state and legacy content intact.
- Show useful errors without discarding form entries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled fields, 44px touch targets, reduced motion, and readable contrast.
