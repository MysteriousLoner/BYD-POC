BYD Seal 6 EV: Futuristic Front Cabin Design

The BYD Seal 6 EV’s front cabin combines sleek design with advanced tech. A 15.6-inch touchscreen seamlessly integrates into the minimalist dashboard, complemented by ambient lighting and blue accents. The multifunction steering wheel and digital display offer easy control, while the ergonomic center console with premium leather ensures comfort. It’s a perfect blend of modern style and cutting-edge functionality

Modern Premium Interior


The BYD Seal 6 EV interior is modern and minimalist, featuring a sleek dashboard with dual digital displays — a large central infotainment screen and a digital driver cluster. It has a flat-bottom steering wheel with integrated controls, premium quilted leather seats, ambient lighting, and a clean center console with cup holders and wireless charging. The cabin emphasizes tech-forward design with a spacious, high-quality finish.

Rear-Wheel Drive, Raising the Standard

Equipped with RWD — uncommon in its segment, it offers precise balance, agile handling, and a refined sporty drive that truly distinguishes it.

BYD’s First: Advanced Suspension Innovation

Enjoy enhanced comfort and precision with BYD’s debut dual ball joint MacPherson front suspension, combined with a rear multi-link setup — ensuring confident handling and a smooth ride every time.

 
 
 

Smart Storage, All Around

The BYD SEAL 6 EV offers a roomy 460L trunk plus a 65L waterproof, odor-resistant frunk — perfect for anything from a 20-inch carry-on to full-size luggage. Versatile, practical, and travel-ready.

 
 
 

Smart Connectivity, Effortless Access
