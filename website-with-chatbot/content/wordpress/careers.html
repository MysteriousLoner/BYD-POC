CAREER WITH US

 

A FUTURE POWERED BY INNOVATION.

BYD Millennium Motors, a key distributor and retail partner for BYD in Malaysia, is at the forefront of the electric vehicle revolution. We are looking for individuals who are passionate about sustainable mobility and future-driven technology. Ideal candidates should bring relevant experience, a growth mindset, and a commitment to delivering exceptional customer experiences as we drive the EV movement forward in Malaysia.

Join our growing team and be part of reshaping the future of mobility!

JUNIOR GRAPHIC DESIGNER

We are seeking a creative and enthusiastic Junior Graphic Designer to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will collaborate closely with the Marketing Department to craft compelling visual content that promotes our automotive brands— BYD and DENZA—as well as support dealership marketing initiatives. This is an excellent opportunity for a detail-oriented designer with a passion for branding and digital media.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Design a wide range of marketing materials including social media graphics, banners, posters, brochures, and EDMs
  • Create digital content for various online platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Xiaohongshu (XHS), and our websites
  • Develop visual assets for showrooms, event backdrops, and promotional displays
  • Ensure all creative outputs are consistent with brand guidelines and corporate identity standards
  • Collaborate with the Marketing team to brainstorm and develop creative concepts
  • Edit photos and videos for marketing and promotional use
  • Provide support on ad-hoc design tasks and special projects as required

WORK LOCATIONS:

  • BYD MILLENNIUM KL NORTH, KL

TIK TOK LIVE HOST

We are on the lookout for a confident, energetic, and engaging Live Streamer/Host to join our growing team. As the face of our online livestream sessions, you will play a key role in promoting BYD and DENZA car models, dealership activities, and special promotions. Your goal will be to drive brand visibility, engage with online audiences, and attract potential customers across multiple platforms.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Host engaging livestream sessions on platforms such as Facebook Live and TikTok Live
  • Present and introduce car models, dealership promotions, and campaigns in a lively and professional manner
  • Collaborate with the marketing team to plan, prepare, and execute livestream content
  • Interact with viewers in real-time, answer questions, and encourage customer enquiries during livestreams
  • Prepare scripts, talking points, and promotional materials ahead of each session
  • Assist with short video shoots, showroom events, and exhibitions as needed
  • Provide feedback and suggestions to improve livestream content and performance

WORK LOCATIONS:

  • BYD MILLENNIUM KL NORTH, KL
  • BYD MILLENNIUM CHERAS, KL
  • BYD MILLENNIUM BANDAR UTAMA, PJ
  • BYD MILLENNIUM IOI, PUTRAJAYA

MARKETING EXECUTIVE (EVENT & BRANDING FOCUS)

We are seeking a creative and enthusiastic Marketing Executive to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will collaborate closely with the Marketing Department to craft compelling visual content that promotes our automotive brands— BYD and DENZA—as well as support dealership marketing initiatives. This is an excellent opportunity for a detail-oriented designer with a passion for branding and digital media.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Plan, coordinate, and execute marketing events, roadshows, and showroom promotions
  • Work closely with vendors, partners, and internal teams to ensure seamless event execution
  • Work closely with designer on event-related materials such as buntings, flyers, and promotional giveaways
  • Assist with showroom branding, layout, and visual merchandising
  • Support the marketing team with basic social media content and paid advertisements when required
  • Monitor and report on event performance, feedback, and budget tracking
  • Perform other marketing-related duties and ad-hoc tasks as needed

  • BYD MILLENNIUM KL NORTH, KL

SALES ADVISOR / SALES CONSULTANT

We are seeking a motivated and customer-focused Sales Advisor to join our growing team. In this role, you will be the face of our brand—engaging with customers, showcasing the innovation behind BYD vehicles, and guiding them through the sales process. This is an excellent opportunity for someone who thrives in a fast-paced environment, enjoys meeting new people, and has a passion for the automotive and EV industry

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Generate sales of new vehicles to prospective customers.
  • Initiating and maintaining relationships with customers through outbound sales activities
  • Taking customers through a thorough needs analysis, making suitable product recommendations and organizing test drives
  • Handle documentation and processes for loan, insurance, registration, and handover
  • Negotiating deals with customers
  • Delivering sold vehicles personally to ensure quality service and to generate referrals
  • Participate in showroom activities, roadshows and promotional campaigns
  • Maintain an exceptional standard of customer service.
  • Commission and Incentive applicable.
  • Able to work on weekends and public holidays
  • Position available at Kuala Lumpur, Seremban 2, Cheras, Bandar Utama
  • Fresh Graduates are welcomed to apply.

WORK LOCATIONS:

  • BYD MILLENNIUM KL NORTH, KL
  • BYD MILLENNIUM CHERAS, KL
  • BYD MILLENNIUM BANDAR UTAMA, PJ
  • BYD MILLENNIUM IOI, PUTRAJAYA
